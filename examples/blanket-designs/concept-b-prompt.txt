Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: single photorealistic top-down catalogue photograph of an original adult sofa throw concept, "Sun Arc" / 日弧. Do not print the product name.

Subject and construction:
One single rectangular, thin, flexible cotton jacquard throw blanket. Proposed body size 140 cm wide by 180 cm tall, body aspect ratio exactly 14:18 excluding fringe. A single continuous piece of woven cloth, no filling, no padding, no quilting and no patchwork seams. This must look like a draping soft cotton throw, not a thick comforter, carpet or rigid board.

Colour and EXACT pattern:
Deep cobalt-blue ground, approximately #294B83. Exactly FOUR enormous, solid, warm apricot-orange semicircles, approximately #E3AA72. Each is a true half-disc with one straight vertical diameter edge and one curved semicircular edge. No complete circles, no rings, no other shapes. Arrange the four semicircles in a spacious 2-column by 2-row grid, with clear uninterrupted blue negative space between all four and blue margins around the pattern.
Top-left semicircle: straight vertical edge on its left, curved bulge pointing right toward the middle.
Top-right semicircle: straight vertical edge on its right, curved bulge pointing left toward the middle.
Bottom-left semicircle: straight vertical edge on its right, curved bulge pointing left toward the outside.
Bottom-right semicircle: straight vertical edge on its left, curved bulge pointing right toward the outside.
Thus the upper pair faces inward and the lower pair faces outward. All four shapes are fully visible, clearly separate, and have equal visual scale. Orange and blue are woven into the same uninterrupted cloth surface, not appliqué, not stitched-together panels.

Edges:
The cloth body has all four complete rectangular corners. Both vertical long edges have a narrow matching blue self-folded hem; there is absolutely NO fringe on either left or right long edge. Only the top and bottom short edges have short, fine, dense natural off-white cotton fringe, proposed about 3 cm long beyond the cloth body. Keep both fringed ends fully visible.

Photographic treatment:
One coherent, high-quality, realistic product photograph. Entire single blanket lying flat, seen precisely from overhead, long axis vertical. All four corners, all four edges, and the full fringe are visible with modest background breathing room. A little soft natural fabric undulation is allowed, but must not conceal or distort the count, orientation or shape of the four semicircles. Subtle visible jacquard yarn grain and tactile cotton softness, not printed smooth plastic. Warm very light grey background and gentle natural side-light. Preserve thin cloth edges and plausible soft drape.
